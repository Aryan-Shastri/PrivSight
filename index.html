Privacy boundary

Useful automation without raw-data exposure.

Local perception

Packaged UI, OCR, and face models process the active viewport inside the extension.

Tokenized fields

Sensitive values remain in a browser-local vault; the planner sees scoped aliases only.

Closed actions

Unknown actions fail closed, stale targets are rejected, and consequential submits require approval.

No raw screenshots, DOM, credentials, cookies, or query strings leave the browser.

Only schema-checked semantic evidence and irreversibly redacted pixels may cross the privacy boundary.
